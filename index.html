Common Questions

Answers before you call

How much does a criminal defense lawyer cost in Dallas?

Most cases are handled on a flat fee that depends on the charge, the court, and whether trial is likely. The initial consultation is free, so you will know what your case involves before you spend anything.

What should I do right after an arrest?

Say that you are choosing to remain silent, ask for a lawyer, and do not consent to searches or discuss your case on jail phones. Then call a defense attorney. Some deadlines, like the 15-day DWI license hearing, start immediately.

Does Peter handle both state and federal charges?

Yes. Peter has defended clients in Texas state courts and in federal court, including the Northern District of Texas, for 31 years.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The initial case review is free and confidential, with no obligation to hire the firm.
