What factors determine if you can be released on bail?

Peter M. Barrett, Attorney 31 years practicing · 150+ jury trials · Dallas, Texas

Facing any type of serious criminal offense can be exceedingly stressful, and it might not always be easy to know what to expect from this process. From the moment you are arrested, you may have questions about your legal rights and options and what you can do to protect your interests as you await future court proceedings.

One question that may be at the front of your mind could pertain to whether you will be eligible to be released on bail while awaiting your court date. Knowing what factors may determine if you can be released on bail after being charged with a serious crime could be integral to preparing for this process.

Examples of factors

There may be numerous types of factors that judges in Texas may consider when determining bail, some of which might include:

  • The charges: One of the first factors that a judge might consider could involve the type and severity of the charges against you. The judge may also choose to consider the strength of the evidence and case against you.
  • Criminal records: Another factor that might impact the court to release you on bail could involve your criminal history. Your chances of getting released on bail may be greater with no previous criminal convictions on record.
  • Flight risk: The court may also seek to determine if there is any evidence of flight risk. Factors such as having ties to the community or having a steady job could help combat such issues.
  • Safety concerns: The topic of safety may also be an integral factor, as a judge may be more likely to grant bail if there is no evidence to suggest that you do not pose a threat to the safety of the community.

Experts also suggest that the court may also enquire about factors such as your employment status and age or even consider aspects such as your physical and mental health when determining bail.

Addressing your situation

These may only be a few types of factors that could determine if a judge will release you on bail when facing serious criminal charges. When facing such issues, it might be helpful to consider speaking to someone with experience in such matters for much-needed guidance in addressing your situation. Such a decision could help you better understand what to expect and help you prepare to create a strategy with which to safeguard your interests through every stage of the criminal justice system process.
