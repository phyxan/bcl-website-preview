Three indirect impacts of a conviction for a sex crime

Peter M. Barrett, Attorney 31 years practicing · 100+ jury trials · Dallas, Texas

Anyone facing allegations of a sex offense is likely aware of the potential criminal penalties, but it is important to discuss the indirect impact that can accompany a conviction. Such a conviction often leads to a complex web of legal and social consequences that extend well beyond the courtroom. Three of the most common are discussed below.

#1: Difficulty getting hired

An employer may request applicants disclose a criminal record. This can serve as a significant hurdle for individuals with a sex offense conviction. Employers may be reluctant to hire someone with this type of record, especially for positions of trust or those involving vulnerable populations. Consequently, individuals may find their professional opportunities severely restricted.

#2: Professional licensure at risk

State licensing boards for professionals such as teachers, healthcare workers, and lawyers often require licenses to practice. Although the rules vary by state, in Texas a conviction may lead to the revocation or suspension of any existing licenses, and a board may outright deny new applications. This impact is particularly acute for individuals who build their careers on such credentials.

#3: Public perception and career progression

The stigma attached to a sex offense conviction is profound and pervasive. It can affect career progression as employers, colleagues, and clients may have reservations about associating with someone who has such a conviction. This can lead to a lack of trust, fewer promotions, and in some cases, complete ostracization from one’s chosen field.

The repercussions of a sex offense conviction on one’s professional life are both severe and enduring. They not only limit immediate job prospects but also affect long-term career goals and professional relationships. It is essential for individuals facing such allegations to understand the legal implications and the breadth of the impact on professional life.

While the road ahead may be difficult, there are resources and support systems in place to assist individuals facing allegations of a sex offense. It is possible not only to fight the charges, but to do so discretely. An attorney experienced in this niche area of criminal law can review your case and discuss your options.
